## 10.5 Group Attack

A Group Attack is a single Attack made by one Unit with
support from other Units surrounding it.
1. Attack Leader: Any Unit chosen to attack a Target Hex.
2. Supporting Units: Any Units within one Hex of the Attack
Leader.

## 10.6 Supporting Units

To support the Attack Leader, a Supporting Unit must meet
all of the following criteria:
• The Supporting Unit is in the Attack Leader’s Hex or one
of its six adjacent Hexes.
• The Target Hex is in the Supporting Unit's Fire Zone (5.0) .
• The Target Hex is within the Supporting Unit's Normal
Range (i.e. not in Long Range).
• The Supporting Unit does not have a Hit Marker affecting
its Firepower.
If the Attack Leader is in Close Combat, only Units in the same
Hex can provide support.

## 10.7 Group Attack Rating

The Attack Leader receives a Group Support Bonus of
+1AR for each Supporting Unit.
All other AR modifiers are based on the Attack Leader.
Group AR = Leader’s AR
+ 1AR per Supporting Unit

## 10.9 Group Rally

Units in the same and/or adjacent hexes may attempt to Rally
together as one Action.
Roll an individual Rally Check (7.7) for each Unit in the
Group. Each Unit that succeeds in its Rally Check removes
its Hit Marker.
Group Rally Action Cost = 5AP

## 10.12 Other Group Actions

for these Group Actions is the same as the highest individual
Unit’s cost.
Groups may use Card Actions that have the Group Action
Icon (8.8) . The card’s Group Action Cost is the Action Cost

## Key mechanics (vision pass)

- **10.0 Group Action:** select a **Group** instead of one Unit; only **one d10 Spent Check** for the whole Group. Steps: select Group → perform Group Action → determine Group Action Cost → one Group Spent Check.
- **10.1:** the Action is one Action (2.3) or Card Action (8.8). A Group is made of **Fresh** Units; **Spent Units may join only if the Cost is 0AP** (3.4). The single Spent Check **affects all Units in the Group**, and a Group Action **Stresses all** participants.
- **10.2 Group Move:** pick any number of Units in the **same or continuously adjacent** hexes. Each may move to an adjacent hex then Pivot, or just Pivot in place; not all must move.
- **10.3 Separating:** Units must **begin** continuously adjacent but **may split apart** during the move.
- **10.4 Group Move Action Cost = the highest individual Move Cost in the Group.** Units may be in the Group without moving — they don't change the cost but **still risk being Spent** if the Group fails its check.

## Example (red box) — Group Move sequence

- (A) **Soviet Turn:** four Rifles move as a Group toward the German HMG. Three move into Open Terrain (1AP) but Rifles 01 moves into Heavy Woods (2AP) → **Group cost = 2AP** (the highest). One Spent Check, roll 5 → all four stay Fresh, Stressed.
- (B) **German Turn:** HMG fires at Rifles 02, scores a Critical Hit, destroys them → placed on the Soviet CAPs Track (7.12).
- (C) **Soviet Turn:** the three survivors are **no longer continuously adjacent**, so they can't move as one Group. Moving Rifles 03+04 (Move 1AP **+1AP Stress** = **2AP** Group cost): one Spent Check, roll 1 → **both become Spent**, Stressed.

### Group Attack & other group rules
- **10.5/10.6 Group Attack:** one **Attack Leader** plus **Supporting Units**. A Unit can support only if it meets **all**: it's in the Leader's hex or one of the 6 adjacent hexes; the Target Hex is in its **Fire Zone** (5.0) and within its **Normal Range** (not Long Range); and it has **no Hit Marker affecting its Firepower**. In Close Combat, only Units in the **same hex** can support.
- **10.7 Group AR = Leader's AR + 1AR per Supporting Unit** (all other AR modifiers come from the Leader).
- **10.8 Group Attack Action Cost = the Attack Leader's Action Cost.**
- **10.9 Group Rally:** Units in same/adjacent hexes Rally as one Action; roll an **individual Rally Check per Unit** (7.7); each success removes that Unit's marker. **Group Rally cost = 5AP.**
- **10.10 Group Spent Check:** use the Group Action Cost + modifiers (Stress, Difficult Terrain, Hit Marker), optionally reduced by CAPs; roll **one** d10. **Fail → every Unit in the Group becomes Spent** (even ones that separated, 10.3); **all are Stressed either way.**
- **10.11 Stress:** applies the +1AP if **any** Group member acted on the player's previous Turn.
- **10.12 Other Group Actions** (Hasty Defense, laying Wire, Smoke, Hiding, etc.): AP cost = **highest individual cost**. Card Actions with the Group icon (8.8) cost the card's printed Action Cost.

## Examples (red boxes)

**Group Attack:** HMG34 (Leader, red 5 FP) attacks Soviet Rifles (12DR).
