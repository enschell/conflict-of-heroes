## Key mechanics (vision pass)

- **16.1 Trucks & Wagons** (Wheeled, move Units/supplies over Roads): **cannot take control of a hex** (4.4); when **destroyed they do not reduce the CAPs Track but do count for VP**; **Trucks may attack only in Close Combat; Wagons may not attack** at all.
- **16.2 Turreted Vehicles** (marked with a **white circle** under the Attack Cost): 360° turret — may attack a hex **outside its Arc of Fire without changing facing**, but pays a **+2AP Attack Cost Penalty** to do so.
- **16.3 Self-Propelled Guns (SPGs):** **not** turreted — may only Attack **within their Arc of Fire** and must take a **Move Action to Pivot** to face a target.
- **16.4 Mobile Vehicles** (have **both** wheel and track bonus symbols): may use them **in any order** onto Roads or Open Terrain; the **track bonus can move out of a Vehicle-occupied hex** (avoiding wheel Congestion).
- **16.5 Open-Topped Vehicles** (marked with a **white field with a red border** under Flank Defense): vs **HE (13.9), Flamethrowers (18.0), red-FP Close Combat, or Sniper** attacks, their **blue Flank Defense is treated as red** and they pull a **Soft Target** Hit Marker. Against any other attack they use their normal **blue Flank Defense**.
- **16.6 APCs:** a **Soft Target being transported by an APC** with a **red shield** icon gains **+2DR APC Bonus from all flanks**.
- **16.7 Field Guns:** Crewed Units (names contain **Gun / PaK / ATG / FlaK**) with **green Wheel Move** costs (can be towed, 15.6).
