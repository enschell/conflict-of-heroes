# 14.0 Smoke

**Apply when:** firing Smoke (on-map or via OBA), and the LOS / defense / attack effects of Heavy vs Light Smoke and how it dissipates.

**Subsections:** 14.0, 14.1, 14.2, 14.3, 14.4

## Key mechanics (vision pass)

- **14.0:** Smoke conceals Units (11.5). It can be fired by **mortars 8cm (80mm)+**, **all Artillery Cards**, **Pioneers (18.1)**, and Mission-specified Tanks, via an Attack Action. It can target any terrain **except Water**. Instead of resolving an Attack, place a **+2DR Heavy Smoke Marker** on the target. **Smoke never damages Units** (friendly or enemy).
- **14.1 Fire Smoke (on-map):** choose a target to Direct/Indirect Attack → place a **Heavy Smoke Marker** instead of attacking → make a **d10 Spent Check** on the Unit's Attack Action Cost.
- **14.2 Fire Smoke (OBA):** note "firing Smoke" when planning the Strike → roll a **Drift Check** on resolution → place Heavy Smoke Markers in the landing hex **and all six adjacent hexes**.
- **14.3 Heavy Smoke effects:** **+2DR** when defending in it; **−2AR** when attacking out of it; **blocks LOS at all elevations**; **+1 Rally bonus** (7.8); **cannot be entered/exited with Bonus Moves** (15.2).
- **14.4 Dissipation:** during **each** Pre-Round Sequence (9.4), **Heavy Smoke flips to Light Smoke (+1DR)**; existing **Light Smoke is removed**.
  - **Light Smoke effects:** **+1DR** defending in it; **−1AR** attacking out of it; **does not block LOS**, but gives **+1DR** if LOS merely passes through it; **LOS is blocked (all elevations) if it passes through two or more** Light Smoke Markers.
- **Stacking cap:** stacked/in-LOS Smoke Markers are limited to a **cumulative +2DR or −2AR** and count as Heavy Smoke (14.3).
