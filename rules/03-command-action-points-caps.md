• Spend up to 2 CAPs before rolling.

## Key mechanics (vision pass)

- **CAPs are a shared pool** for all of a side's Units during the Round; allocation per Round comes from the Mission. Spending a CAP moves the marker down one space; at 0 you have none left. CAPs replenish at the start of each Round (9.7). Track runs 0–15.
- **3.2 Modify a d6 Number Check:** spend **up to 2 CAPs before rolling**; each CAP raises *or* lowers the target Number by 1. Applies to Attack Hit Number (6.8), Rally Number (7.7), Initiative Check Number (9.11), or any d6 number check.
- **3.3 Modify a d10 Spent Check:** spend **any number of CAPs before rolling**; each CAP **reduces the Action Cost by 1**.
- **3.4 0AP Action Cost:** if cost is reduced to **0AP, no Spent Check is made**. A **Spent Unit may take a 0AP Action** (and remains Spent afterward).

**Spent Check Reference Table** (Action Cost → chance of becoming Spent):

| Action Cost | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spent Chance | 20% | 30% | 50% | 60% | 80% | 90% | 100% |

*(Consistent with the Spent Die faces [1,1,2,3,3,4,5,5,6,7]: chance = P(roll ≤ cost).)*

## Examples (red boxes)

**Reducing Action Cost — German Turn:** MG34 attacks the Spent Soviet Rifles and misses for a 2AP Action Cost (would need 3+ to stay Fresh). Holding 5 CAPs, before rolling they spend **1 CAP to reduce the cost 2AP→1AP** (3.3), then roll a 2 and remain Fresh (now Stressed). This lowered the Spent chance from 30% to 20%.

**Spent Unit Action — Soviet Turn:** The **Spent** Rifles want to fire back. Soviets spend **4 of their 5 CAPs to reduce the 4AP Attack Cost to 0AP**, which lets the Spent Unit act (3.4). They miss, remain Spent, and are Stressed. **German Turn:** the Fresh MG34 moves adjacent for a 2AP cost (1AP Move + 1AP Stress); to guarantee staying Fresh they spend **2 CAPs to reduce it to 0AP** — MG34 moves, makes no Spent Check, stays Fresh, still Stressed.
