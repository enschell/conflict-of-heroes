## 7.3 Hit Marker Effects

Hit Markers apply Modifiers to a Unit’s stats and abilities.
Each Modifier printed on a Hit Marker applies to the stat in the
corresponding location on the Unit.
A +1 in the top left corner of a Hit Marker means the Hit Unit’s
Attack Cost is increased by 1AP.
A symbol means the Unit may not use the corresponding
Action at all. A symbol in the top left corner of a Hit Marker
means the Unit may no longer Attack.
If no value appears in the location of a Hit Marker, there is no
effect on the corresponding stat of the Hit Unit.
Taking a Hit does not necessarily mean that men are killed.
In battle, soldiers often lose Unit cohesion, resulting in them
freezing or running away. At other times, they may be injured
or mentally unable to continue to fight effectively. Modern
combat effects are simulated through the Hit Markers.

## 7.4 Receiving a Second Hit

If a Unit with a Hit Marker receives another Hit, it is
destroyed (7.11) .

## 7.6 Rally Action

A Hit Unit may attempt to remove its Hit Marker by Rallying.

## 7.7 Rally Check

A Rally Number is located at the top center of most Hit
Markers.
To Rally, resolve a 2d6 Rally Check equal to or higher than
the Hit Marker’s Rally Number.
If the Rally Check is successful, the Hit Marker is revealed
and returned to the Draw Pile.
If the Rally Check fails, the Hit Marker is not revealed. It may
attempt another Rally Check on future Turns.

## 7.8 Rally Number Modifiers

A Unit’s Rally Number is lowered by Terrain, Fortifications,
and other friendly Units in its Hex. These modifiers are
cumulative.

## 7.9 Rally Restrictions

A Hit Unit may not take a Rally Action if either:
• It is in a Hex with an enemy Unit.
• Its Hit Marker is marked with an ‘XX’ or ‘No Rally’.

## Key mechanics (vision pass) — hits

- **7.0 Hit:** a successful Attack inflicts a Hit.
- **7.1 Critical Hit:** if the Attack roll **exceeds the Hit Number by 4 or more**, the Target is **instantly destroyed** (7.11).
- **7.2 Hit Markers:** if the roll is **≥ Hit Number but not a Critical Hit**, the Hit Unit **randomly pulls a Hit Marker** — a **red Soft Target** marker if the Hit was vs red Defense, a **blue Armored Target** marker if vs blue Defense. Look at it secretly, place it **face-down under the Unit** (you may inspect your own anytime).
- **7.3 Hit Marker effects:** each printed modifier applies to the stat in the **matching location** on the counter. **`+1` top-left = Attack Cost +1AP.** A **⊘** means that Action can't be used at all (⊘ top-left = can no longer Attack). Blank location = no effect. A blue/red number affects only the **same-colored** stat.
- **7.4 Second Hit:** a Unit that already has a Hit Marker and takes **another Hit is destroyed** (7.11).
- **7.5 Revealing:** reveal a Hit Marker when it affects the Unit's **Attack/Move Cost for a Spent Check** (2.4) or **Defense/Attack Rating in Combat** (6.1, 6.6); then it stays **face-up / public**.

## Key mechanics (vision pass) — rallying & destruction

- **7.6/7.7 Rally:** a Hit Unit may **Rally** to remove its Hit Marker. Resolve a **2d6 Rally Check ≥ the marker's Rally Number** (top-center of the marker). Success → marker revealed and returned to the Draw Pile; failure → not revealed, may retry on future Turns.
- **7.8 Rally Number modifiers (cumulative):** lower the Rally Number by **1** for each of: occupying Light/Heavy Woods, Wood/Stone Buildings, Walls (6.5) or Fortifications (17.0), Heavy Smoke (14.3); **and by 1 per friendly un-Hit Unit** in the hex. You may also spend **up to 2 CAPs** before rolling.
- **7.9 Rally restrictions:** **cannot Rally** if sharing a hex with an **enemy Unit**, or if the marker shows **XX / "No Rally"**.
- **7.10 Rally Spent Check:** after a Rally Action the Unit **must make a Spent Check regardless** of the Rally result. **Rally Action Cost = 5AP + modifiers** (e.g. Stress).
- **7.11 Destroyed Units:** remove from the Map and place on the owner's **CAPs Track** (7.12); reveal/return its Hit Marker; adjust VP if the Mission says so (9.1).
- **7.12 Reduce CAPs:** the 1st destroyed Unit sits on the **starting CAP** number, the 2nd on the next lower, etc. → **CAP allocation drops by 1 per destroyed Unit** for the rest of the Mission. If the CAPs marker is already on that space, it's pushed down one and that CAP is lost immediately.
- **7.13 Minimum CAPs:** once only **3 open spaces** remain on the track, destroyed Units stop being placed there — a player always starts a new Round with **at least 3 CAPs**.
