## Game Overview

Conflict of Heroes is a tactical war game of platoon- to company-sized missions. Each **Mission** portrays an actual historical engagement with different Objectives worth **Victory Points (VPs)**. The player with VP Advantage at the end of the Mission wins.

A Mission consists of several **Rounds**. During a Round, you and your opponent alternate **Turns**. On your Turn you take a single **Action**, such as moving a Unit to an adjacent Hex, attacking a Target, Rallying a Hit Unit, or playing a Battle Card.

As Units take Actions they eventually become **Spent** and can take no further Actions. As the Round progresses, both players have fewer and fewer Units available, and both must Pass to start the next Round and refresh their Units.

In addition to Units, each player has another key resource: **Command Action Points (CAPs)**. You receive a limited number each Round, representing your force's leadership and logistics support. CAPs can be spent to take Actions with Spent Units, or to adjust the odds of *any* die roll you make. Deciding how to spend CAPs — assisting a frontline Unit during an important attack vs. helping a panicked Unit rally — is a key component of winning.

## A Note for Returning Gamers (3rd Edition changes)

The 3rd Edition rules change several key elements from previous editions:

- A Unit **no longer receives 7 Action Points**. Instead, each Turn a player selects one Unit to take an Action. After completing the Action, they roll a custom **d10 die** to determine if the Unit becomes **Spent**.
- If the player rolls **higher than the Action's Cost**, the Unit is free to continue taking Actions on future Turns. More involved Actions have a higher Spent probability.
- On average, Units take about the same number of Actions as in the previous 7AP edition, but **now Units may not always perform as expected**.
- This and other new mechanics like **Stress** encourage integrated tactics using multiple Units together, and heighten the "Fog of War": players must assess the risk attached to each Action and time their CAPs to mitigate it.
- All previous Conflict of Heroes editions are **100% compatible** with these 3rd Edition rules; the game can also be played with previous-edition rules.
- Living rules: www.AcademyGames.com/AtB

## How to Use This Rulebook

The rulebook is arranged into **Sections**, the first consisting of Chapters 1–9. Each Section teaches the rules needed to play the Missions listed at the end of that Section. Early Missions involve core rules (moving, attacking); later Missions introduce additional mechanisms and Units with unique capabilities.

First-time players: rather than reading everything at once, read Chapters 1–9 until you reach the red box that says you are **ready to play Mission 1**. After playing Mission 1, read up to the next red box, which indicates you have learned the additional rules required for the next two Missions.

**Box conventions (important for examples):**
- **Red boxes** provide illustrated **step-by-step examples** of game play. *(These are transcribed in each chapter file under an "Examples (red boxes)" heading — use them as worked reference implementations of the rules.)*
- **Blue boxes** provide **Designer Notes** on rules logic, historical unit capabilities, and design philosophy. *(Captured under "Designer notes (blue boxes)" where present.)*
