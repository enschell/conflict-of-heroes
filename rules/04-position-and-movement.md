## 4.0 Unit Position

Units can only occupy Full Hexes on the Map.
Half-hexes at the edge of the Map are never playable.

## 4.1 Unit Facing

A Unit’s Front direction is indicated by the green field along
its top edge.
A Unit must face one of the six sides of the Hex it occupies.
It cannot face a corner of the Hex.

The direction a Unit is facing is critical. It indicates where the
Unit’s ‘focus’ is and what positions its soldiers are taking.
While deployed behind a wall or against an embankment,
a Unit is very vulnerable to fire coming from its Flank. Not
only is the Unit often more exposed, but Flank attacks can
be psychologically devastating. ‘Where did the enemy come
from? Are we surrounded? Can we get away?’
The natural instinct is often to run away from the threat or
to hunker down. A Unit’s experience and leadership play a
key role in assessing the new threat, keeping Unit cohesion,
and conducting an effective reaction.

## 4.3 Unit Stacking

Units may occupy the same Hex as friendly or enemy Units.
Units occupying the same Hex are said to be Stacked.
There is no limit to the number of Units that can occupy a
single Hex and these Units may be facing different directions.

## 4.4 Control Markers

Control Markers are placed on the Map during
Mission setup to indicate key Objective Hexes.
The side of the Control Marker that is face-up
indicates which side currently controls the Hex.
If the opposing side gains control of the Hex,
flip the Control Marker over to show the new
controlling side’s symbol.
A Control Marker changes sides if BOTH of the
following are true:
• The controlling side has no Units in the Objective Hex.
• The opposing side has a Unit with positive Firepower
(either red or blue) in the Objective Hex, even if just
moving through.

## 4.5 Foot Unit Movement

A Unit is considered a Foot Unit if it has a
red Move Cost in its top right corner.
When a Foot Unit performs a Move
Action, it may move from its current Hex
to any adjacent Hex.
After moving into a new Hex, the Unit may
freely Pivot, for no extra AP cost, to face any of the six sides
of the new Hex. The Unit may not Pivot before moving.

A Unit may never exit the Map, unless specified by a Mission.

## 4.6 Pivoting in Place

A Unit may use a Move Action to Pivot in Place, rotating as
far as it wants, without moving into a new Hex.
1AP Cost for any type of Unit to Pivot in Place.

## 4.7 Move Cost Modifiers

A Unit that makes a difficult move is more likely to become
Spent as a result.
This is represented by adding Move Cost Penalties to its
Action Cost.
Move Cost Penalties for Foot Units & Field Guns:
• Difficult Terrain (4.9)
• Backwards Move (4.11)
• Elevation Move (12.2)

## 4.9 Difficult Terrain

A Unit moving into a Hex with Difficult Terrain suffers a
Move Cost Penalty.
Common Difficult Terrain Move Penalties for Foot Units:
• Wall: +1AP for crossing a wall Hexside.
• Heavy Woods: +1AP to move into Hex.
• Wood or Stone Buildings: +1AP to move into Hex.
• Water: +5AP to move into Hex.
Units moving out of Difficult Terrain or just Pivoting in place
(4.6) do not receive any Move Penalties. Only add these
penalties when moving into a new Difficult Terrain Hex.

## Key mechanics (vision pass)

- **Hexes:** Units occupy **Full Hexes** only; edge half-hexes are unplayable (two half-hexes from adjacent boards form one playable Full Hex). A Hex ≈ 50 m across.
- **Facing (4.1):** six sides; the **green field** edge is the Front. A Unit cannot face a corner. **Front Hexes** = the faced hex + front-left + front-right. **Flank Hexes** = the opposite hex + back-left + back-right.
- **Stacking (4.3):** **no limit** to Units in a Hex; stacked Units may face different directions; may be friendly or enemy.
- **Control Marker flip (4.4):** flips to the opposing side **only if BOTH**: (a) controlling side has **no Units** in the Objective Hex, **and** (b) the opposing side has a Unit with **positive Firepower (red or blue)** there — even if just moving through.
- **Foot Unit (4.5):** any Unit with a **red Move Cost**. A Move Action moves it to **any adjacent Hex**; it may **freely Pivot after** moving (no AP), but **may not Pivot before** moving. A Unit may **never exit the Map** unless a Mission says so.
- **Pivot in Place (4.6):** **1AP** for any Unit type; rotate as far as wanted without changing Hex.
- **Difficult Terrain penalties (4.9), applied only when moving INTO the hex** (not when moving out, and not when pivoting):
  | Terrain | Foot Unit penalty |
  |---|---|
  | Cross a Wall hexside | +1AP |
  | Heavy Woods | +1AP |
  | Wood or Stone Building | +1AP |
  | Water | +5AP |
- **Terrain types (4.8):** Open, Plowed Fields (inhibit tracked vehicles; wheeled vehicles prohibited), Water (Foot Units only; may not attack while in water), Walls (protect Units directly behind, follow a hexside, don't change the hex's terrain type), Light Woods, Heavy Woods, Wood Buildings, Stone Buildings (the last four give defense — see 6.4). The terrain under the **center dot** sets the whole hex's type; other art is decorative.
- **Roads (4.10):** moving **from a Road hex to an adjacent Road hex** lets Units **ignore Difficult Terrain penalties**. Roads work regardless of the center dot.
- **Backwards Move (4.11):** moving into one of your own **Flank Hexes** adds **+1AP** (stacks with other penalties); the Unit may still freely Pivot afterward.
- **Entering the Mission (4.12):** moving onto the Map costs **0AP and never needs a Spent Check**, but the Unit **still gets Stressed**. Entry is onto a Mission-specified full entry Hex. Reinforcements may enter as a Group on same/adjacent entry hexes (no Spent Check). If enemy Units occupy **all** entry Hexes, the reinforcement may enter along the map edge **within 2 Hexes** of those enemy Units.

## Examples (red boxes)

**Move into Heavy Woods + Pivot:** Soviet Rifles move forward into a Heavy Woods Hex then Pivot. Move Cost 1AP **+1AP** (Heavy Woods is Difficult Terrain, 4.9); pivoting after the move is free (4.5) → **total 2AP**.

**Movement on Road (A):** Soviet Rifles move from an Open+Road hex to a Heavy Woods+Road hex. Moving **along the Road negates** the +1AP Difficult Terrain penalty → total **1AP**.

**Movement on Road (B):** German Rifles move from an Open hex (no road) into a Heavy Woods+Road hex. Because they did **not start and end on a Road**, they add **+1AP** for Difficult Terrain → total **2AP**.
