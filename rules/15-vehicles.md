## 15.2 Vehicle Bonus Moves

Each Bonus Move symbol (Wheel or Track ) on a Vehicle
counter allows it to move one additional Hex per Move Action
without increasing its Move Cost .
After each Bonus Move, the Vehicle may freely Pivot, for no
extra AP cost.
Wheel Bonus Moves may only be used to move:
• along one Road Hex to an adjacent Road Hex
Track Bonus Moves may be used to move:
• along one Road Hex to an adjacent Road Hex
• into Open Terrain
Wheel Bonus Moves MAY NOT be used to move:
• into a Vehicle-occupied Road hex (Road Congestion)
Bonus Moves MAY NOT be used to enter or exit:
• Difficult Terrain (15.3)
• +2DR Heavy Smoke (14.3)
• any Fortification (17.0) or Obstacle (17.7) marked
with symbols
• move Backwards (4.11) or Pivot in Place (4.6)
A vehicle forfeits ALL Bonus Moves if its first Move
Action was into any of the conditions listed
above.

A Vehicle’s Move Action includes all Bonus Moves. The entire
multi-Hex move is completed before the player’s Turn ends.
A Soviet T-34 has two Track Bonus Move symbols. It
may move a total of three Hexes on Road, Open, Corn, or
Wheat Terrain for a 1AP Cost Move Action.

## 15.8 Transporting (Towing) Units

While a Vehicle is Transporting or Towing a Unit, the Vehicle
and Transported/Towed Units must take Group Actions (10.0) .
The Vehicle and Transported/Towed Units move together for
the Transporting Vehicle’s move cost and may use that
Vehicle’s Bonus Moves.
Transport (Tow) Cost = Vehicle's Move Cost
The Transporting (Towing) Vehicle:
• May Move, Attack, Rally, Stall, and Hide.
The Transported (Towed) Unit:
• May NOT Attack from the Vehicle.
• May NOT use any of its unique abilities (18.1) .
• May Rally and Stall.
The Transporting and Transported Units make a Group
Spent Check that affects both Units (10.10) .
Ex: A hit Rifles Unit, loaded on an APC, successfully
Rallies. They then fail their 5AP Spent Check and both
the Rifles and APC become Spent, and both are Stressed.
Ex: An APC transporting Pioneers (18.1) enters and triggers
a Mine Hex. Both Units are attacked by the Mines.

## 15.10 Towing Vehicles

Immobilized or Stunned Vehicles (15.13) may be towed by
another Vehicle, which may use its Bonus Moves.
• Wheeled Units may be towed by any Vehicle.
• Tracked Units may only be towed by Tracked Vehicles.
The towed Vehicle may not Fire.

## 15.11 Attacking Transport Vehicles

When resolving an Attack against a Transporting Vehicle,
first roll an Attack against the Transported Unit, then against
the Transport Vehicle.
• Both the Transporting and Transported Units receive all
Terrain Defense Modifiers for the Hex.
If the Transport Vehicle is destroyed, the transported Unit
is immediately Unloaded at no cost, and is placed in the Hex
facing any direction.

## 15.15 Vehicles as Cover

Foot Units gain a +1DR Vehicle Cover Bonus when
occupying the same Hex as any friendly Vehicle (but not

## Key mechanics (vision pass)

- **15.0:** Vehicles use the **Foot Unit movement rules (Section 4)** plus the additions below.
- **15.1 Wheeled vs Tracked (by Move Cost color, top-right):** **Green Move Cost = Wheeled**, **Blue Move Cost = Tracked**. The number of **Wheel/Track symbols** below the Move Cost = how many **Bonus Moves** (15.2) it gets.
- **15.2 Bonus Moves:** each symbol lets the Vehicle move **one extra hex per Move Action at no added Move Cost** (free Pivot after each). 
  - **Wheel** bonus moves: only **Road→adjacent Road**.
  - **Track** bonus moves: **Road→adjacent Road, or into Open Terrain**.
  - Bonus Moves **may not**: enter a Vehicle-occupied Road hex (**Road Congestion**, wheel only); enter/exit Difficult Terrain (15.3), **+2DR Heavy Smoke**, or any Fortification/Obstacle marked with wheel/track symbols; move Backwards (4.11) or Pivot in Place (4.6).
  - A Vehicle **forfeits ALL Bonus Moves** if its **first** move was into any of those barred conditions. The whole multi-hex move (regular + bonus) resolves **before the Turn ends**.
- **15.3 Difficult & Impassable Terrain:**
  | Terrain | Wheeled | Tracked |
  |---|---|---|
  | Light Woods | +2AP | +1AP |
  | Heavy Woods | **Impassable** | +2AP |
  | Wood Buildings | +2AP | +1AP |
  | Stone Buildings | +3AP | +2AP |
  | Plowed Fields | **Impassable** | +0AP (Difficult for Bonus Moves only) |
  | Walls | **Impassable** | +0AP (Difficult for Bonus Moves only) |
  - **Impassable to ALL Vehicles:** **Water**, **Steep Terrain** (12.2).
- **15.4 Vehicles on Roads:** moving **Road→adjacent Road** lets a Vehicle **ignore both Difficult and Impassable Terrain** (for regular **and** Bonus Moves).
- **15.5 Stacking / overrun:** Vehicles may move into/out of hexes with friendly or enemy Units. A Vehicle that **moves through** an enemy hex (in and out the same Turn via Bonus Moves) **cannot be engaged in Close Combat** by the opponent.

## Example (red box)

A Soviet **T-34** with **two Track bonus symbols** may move a total of **three hexes** on Road, Open, Corn, or Wheat terrain for a **1AP** Move Action.

### Transport / towing
- **15.6:** a Vehicle may **transport one Foot Unit** **or** **tow one Field Gun** (16.7).
- **15.7 Loading (limbering)** — a Group Move; **Load cost = loading Unit's Move Cost**:
  - **Same hex:** pay the Move Cost to load, **ignoring Hit Markers and Terrain penalties, but not Stress**.
  - **Adjacent hex:** pay the Move Cost into the Vehicle's hex **including Difficult Terrain and Stress**, then load for free.
  - Place the Unit on the Vehicle facing the same way; one **Group Spent Check**.
- **15.8 Transporting (towing):** the pair must take **Group Actions (10.0)**, moving for the **Vehicle's Move Cost** and using its **Bonus Moves**. **Tow cost = Vehicle's Move Cost.** The **Vehicle** may Move/Attack/Rally/Stall/Hide; the **carried Unit may NOT Attack** and may **NOT use unique abilities (18.1)**, but **may Rally and Stall**. One **Group Spent Check** affects both (10.10).
- **15.9 Unloading (unlimbering)** — a Group Move; **Stunned Units cannot Unload**; **Unload cost = unloading Unit's Move Cost**. Place the Unit under the Vehicle or any adjacent hex (any facing); it pays Move Cost into the entered hex (Terrain + Stress apply); one Group Spent Check.

## Examples (red boxes)

**Vehicle Bonus Movement (4 turns):**
- (A) T-34 moves N into **Heavy Woods** → **forfeits all Bonus Moves** (first move into Difficult Terrain): `1AP + 2AP = 3AP`.
- (B) Wheeled Truck 35 moves N along a Road, using its **1 wheel bonus** for an extra Road hex: 1AP.
- (C) T-34's first move is into **non-Difficult** terrain, so it keeps its **two track bonuses** → moves 3 hexes NE through Open Terrain: 1AP. (Moving into the spent Truck's hex is **not** Congestion — that's wheel-only.)
- (D) Truck 35 moves NW but a spent Truck 36 ahead causes **Road Congestion**, so it can't use its wheel bonus and must wait: 1AP. *(Alternating Units avoided Stress.)*

**Vehicle Moving Uphill:** A German Panzer (1 track bonus) climbs an Open-Terrain hill in one Move Action: base 1AP **+1AP L0→L1 (A) +1AP L1→L2 (B)** (12.2) = **3AP**; Spent Check roll 4 → success, Stressed.

### Vehicle combat
- **15.10 Towing damaged vehicles:** **Immobilized or Stunned** Vehicles may be towed (tower may use its Bonus Moves). **Wheeled** can be towed by any Vehicle; **Tracked only by Tracked**. The towed Vehicle **may not Fire**.
- **15.11 Attacking a Transport:** resolve the Attack **against the carried Unit first, then the Vehicle**; both get the hex's Terrain Defense Modifiers. If the Vehicle is **destroyed**, the carried Unit is **Unloaded free** into the hex (any facing).
- **15.12/15.13:** Vehicles use the normal Combat rules (Sec. 5–7); **Armored Target Hits work like Soft Target Hits**.
- **15.14:** Vehicles get **no Defensive Terrain Bonus in Close Combat** (unlike Foot Units).
- **15.15 Vehicles as Cover:** a Foot Unit sharing a hex with **any friendly Vehicle** (not while being transported) gains **+1DR Vehicle Cover** (stacks with terrain).
- **15.16:** Tanks **may not attack *down* Steep Terrain** into an adjacent hex (they **may** attack **up** it).
