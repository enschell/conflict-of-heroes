## 2.0 Round Sequence

Each Round, players alternate taking Turns.
The player who has the Initiative takes the first Turn.
On your Turn, take a single Action or Pass.
The Round ends when both players Pass consecutively (2.7) .
Each Mission specifies which side has the Initiative in the
first Round. For subsequent Rounds, Initiative is determined
during the Pre-Round Sequence (9.11) .

## 2.6 Stress

A Unit must add a +1AP Stress Penalty to its Action Cost if
it took any Action on its player’s previous Turn.
Stress is not cumulative. A Unit taking two or more consecutive
Actions will still only have a total +1AP Stress Penalty each
Turn.
Stress represents a squad’s or vehicle crew’s mental and
physical state, as well as logistical constraints, that can
affect its Unit cohesion and effectiveness.
Constant action, without pause or support, into known or
unknown danger exerts tremendous stress on participants
that can be highly detrimental to their combat effectiveness.
This includes enemy engagement, real or imagined
ammunition depletion, injured comrades, poor situational
awareness, and fear – all of which amplify a Unit’s
propensity to stall, freeze, or fall back.

## 2.7 Passing

You may Pass, instead of taking an Action on your Turn.
Passing has no cost, no Spent Checks are made, and no
Units are Stressed. Any Stress Marker on a Unit is removed.
Passing does not prevent you from taking Actions on future
Turns.
However, if both players Pass consecutively, the Round
ends (9.0) .

## 2.8 Stall Action

Instead of Passing, one of your Units may take a Stall Action
for an Action Cost of 1AP.
A Unit that Stalls does nothing, but must make a d10 Spent
Check and is Stressed.
Stalling allows a player to assess the next enemy Action
without Passing. But stalling a Unit during combat can
increase its propensity to not move or continue to actively
engage the enemy. This is simulated by Stressing the Unit.
Passing allows you to keep your Units Fresh while seeing
what your opponent does – but there is the danger that your
opponent will also Pass, ending the Round.
Passing may be useful to you if you want the game to
progress quickly. However, if you still have Actions you wish
to take, you may not want to risk having the Round end,
instead choosing to Stall with one of your Units.

## Key mechanics (vision pass — useful for code/tests)

- **Spent Die:** a weighted d10 with face values **[1, 1, 2, 3, 3, 4, 5, 5, 6, 7]**.
- **Spent Check rule (2.5):** after determining the (modified) Action Cost, roll the Spent Die. **Roll > Action Cost → Unit stays Fresh.** **Roll ≤ Action Cost → Unit becomes Spent** (flip to red-bar side). A 3AP Action ≈ 50% Spent chance.
- **Stress (2.6):** add **+1AP** to Action Cost if the Unit took **any** Action on the player's **previous** Turn. Stress is **not cumulative** — max +1AP per Turn regardless of how many consecutive Actions.
- **Passing (2.7):** no cost, no Spent Check, no Stress applied, and **any existing Stress Marker is removed**. Both players Passing consecutively ends the Round (9.0).
- **Stall Action (2.8):** Action Cost **1AP**; the Unit does nothing but **must make a Spent Check and is Stressed**. Used to avoid ending the Round when you have no other Action you want to take.
- **Unit counter layout:** Attack Cost = top-left; Move Cost = top-right; Unit number + name = top-center; combat ratings (Firepower red/blue, Range, Defense) along the bottom (detailed in 6.x).

## Examples (red boxes — worked rules implementations)

**Move Action (A) — German Turn:** Germans move their MG34 forward one Hex. The MG34's Move Cost is 1AP, unaffected by modifiers, so it must roll **2 or higher** to remain Fresh. It rolls a 4 → succeeds (stays Fresh).

**Attack Action (B) — Soviet Turn:** Soviet Rifles attack the MG34 and miss. Attack Cost is 4AP with no modifiers → Action Cost 4AP. The Rifles must roll **higher than 4**; they roll a 3 → fail → flipped to Spent.

**Stress (C) — German Turn:** The MG34 attacks from its new position and misses. It had moved on the previous German Turn, so it is Stressed: Attack Cost 2AP **+1AP Stress = 3AP** modified. Must roll **higher than 3**; rolls a 3 → fail → Spent, and remains Stressed.

**Passing — full Round-end sequence:**
- (A) German Turn: Germans Pass (satisfied with Pioneer position). No German Unit is Stressed.
- (B) Soviet Turn: Rifles move into a Light Woods Hex for 1AP, fail the Spent Check → Spent and Stressed.
- (C) German Turn: Fresh Pioneers attack the Rifles and miss; fail their 2AP Spent Check → Spent and Stressed.
- Soviet Turn: no Fresh Units → Pass. German Turn: no Fresh Units → Pass. Both Passed consecutively → **Round ends, and no Units are Stressed.**

**Stalling — (A) Redo:** If, instead of Passing, the Pioneers wanted to avoid risking the Round ending, they Stall for 1AP, make a successful Spent Check, and are Stressed (Round does not end).
