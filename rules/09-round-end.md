## 9.0 Ending the Round

After both players Pass consecutively, the Round ends.
1. Award End of Round Victory Points (VPs).
2. Determine if the Mission Ends.

## 9.1 Victory Points (VPs)

Victory Points (VPs) are gained, either during or at the end
of a Round, as specified by each Mission, including:
• Destroying enemy Units during a Round (7.11) .
• Controlling Control Markers at the end of a Round (4.4) .
• Achieving Objectives during Mission-specified Scoring
Rounds or when specific Mission Cards are drawn (8.6) .
If you destroy your own Unit (yes, it can happen), your
opponent receives the VP for its destruction.

## 9.5 Advance Round Marker

Advance the Round Marker forward one space on the
Mission Round Track.

## 9.6 Flip Spent Units

Flip all Spent Units on the Map to their Fresh side, retaining
any Hit Markers that are present.
Make sure the Unit is facing the same direction after it is
flipped.

## 9.7 Reset CAPs

Each player resets their CAPs Marker to their allocated
CAPs specified by the Mission, minus 1CAP for each of their
destroyed Units. Reminder: CAPs are never reset below 3.
CAPs left over from the previous Round do not carry over.

## 9.8 Draw Battle Cards

Each player draws the number of Battle Cards specified by
the Mission from the Battle Draw Deck.

## 9.9 Prepare Reinforcements

If the Mission specifies that Reinforcement Units are
available in the coming Round, they should be placed next
to the Map to show that they are now available.
They do not have to be moved onto the Map in the Round
they come into play, but may be held back for a later Round.

## 9.10 Artillery and Smoke Strikes

Artillery and Smoke are not available until Mission 4, and are
discussed in later sections (13.4, 14.0) .

## 9.11 Roll for Initiative

The side that DOES NOT have VP Advantage rolls a 2d6
Initiative Check and may modify it with up to 2CAPs.
The Initiative Check succeeds on a roll of 7 or higher and,
if successful, that player takes the first Turn this Round. If the
roll fails, their opponent takes the first Turn this Round.

## Key mechanics (vision pass)

**9.0 Ending the Round** (after both players Pass consecutively): (1) award End-of-Round VPs; (2) check if the Mission ends.

**9.1 Victory Points** are gained per the Mission: destroying enemy Units in a Round (7.11), controlling Control Markers at Round end (4.4), and achieving Objectives on Scoring Rounds / when Mission Cards are drawn (8.6). *Destroying your own Unit gives the VP to your opponent.*

**9.2 "No-Tie" VP Track.** A **single** track shared by both players. The VP Marker shows a German Cross on one face and a Soviet Star on the other; the **face-up symbol holds VP Advantage**. The side **with** advantage gaining a VP moves the marker **forward**; the side **without** gaining a VP moves it **backward**. Below 1 it **flips** to the other side — **there is no zero; one side always holds VP Advantage**.

**9.3 Mission End.** On the last Round, or when Victory Conditions are met, the Mission ends and the side with **VP Advantage wins**; otherwise continue to the next Pre-Round Sequence.

**9.4 Pre-Round Sequence (in order):**
1. Advance the Round Marker (9.5)
2. Remove all **Light Smoke**
3. Flip all **Heavy Smoke → Light Smoke**
4. Flip all **Spent Units → Fresh** (9.6)
5. **Reset CAPs** (9.7)
6. **Draw Battle Cards** (9.8)
7. **Prepare Reinforcements** (9.9)
8. **Plan** next Round's off-board Artillery Strikes
9. **Resolve** last Round's planned Artillery Strikes
10. **Roll for Initiative** (9.11)

- **9.6 Flip Spent Units:** flip to Fresh but **keep any Hit Markers** and the **same facing**.
- **9.7 Reset CAPs:** to allocated CAPs **minus 1 per destroyed Unit**, but **never below 3**; leftover CAPs **do not carry over**.
- **9.10:** Artillery and Smoke aren't available until Mission 4 (13.4, 14.0).
- **9.11 Initiative:** the side **without VP Advantage** rolls a **2d6 Initiative Check** (modifiable by up to 2 CAPs); **succeeds on 7+** → that side takes the first Turn; on failure the **opponent** goes first.

## Example (red box)

**VP flip:** Germans hold 2 VP. Soviets take an Objective worth 2 VP → the marker moves down to 1, then **flips to the Soviet side**, showing the Soviets now hold a **1 VP advantage**.
