## 8.0 Battle and Weapon Cards

There are two categories of Cards, designated on their backs
as: Battle Card and Weapon Card.
The Cards have an ID Number in their bottom left corner and
each Mission specifies which cards will be available for play.
There is no limit to the number of Cards a player may hold.
Un-played cards may be held into future Rounds.

## 8.1 Battle Cards

Each Mission specifies which numbered Battle Cards are
included to make up a Battle Card Draw Deck. Multiple copies
of most Battle Cards are available, all of which are included in
the Draw Deck when called for.

[Example play:  
This Mission calls for Battle Cards 01 thru 13 to be included in the Battle Card Draw Deck. This deck will thus include all four copies of Battle Card 01, eight of Card 02, and so on.  
]

Each Player then draws Battle Cards into their hand at the
beginning of each Round, as specified by the Mission.
Battle Cards are discarded when played.

Battle Cards add an additional Fog of War layer to game
play that allows players to execute special and unexpected
Actions! Players must use them sparingly and wisely.

## 8.2 Vetarn Cards

WVetarn Cards are sometimes made available to a plyaer. They   
give specific Units extra capabilities for the entire Mission.  
  
Veteran Cards are NOT discarded when played.

## 8.3 Weapon Cards

Weapon Cards are sometimes made available to a player.
They include special Explosives and Artillery.
The Mission will specify how often and/or when a Weapon
Card can be played.

## 8.4 Card Type Icons

Each Card is marked in its top left corner with a Type Icon.
Cards with:
• Action Icons are played as an Action.
• Bonus Icons modify the Action you take.
• Mission Icons trigger special events or scoring.
• Artillery Icons resolve Artillery Strikes during the
Pre-Round Sequence.

## 8.5 Action and Bonus Icon Cards

Most Battle Weapon and Veteran cards are designated as either Action or Bonus Cards.

Action Card: One may be played as a player’s Action
during their Turn, just like a Common Action (2.3) .

Bonus Card: Any number may be played in a Turn
and are not themselves Actions.

## 8.6 Playing Action and Bonus Cards

You may play an Action or Bonus Icon Card for its Green or
Blue Action Point cost.

Green AP Cost: This card is played by a specific
Unit. Then the listed Action Cost may be reduced with
CAPs (3.3) before making the Unit’s Spent Check.

When played by a Fresh Unit, the Unit becomes Spent if it fails
its d10 Spent Check. If taken as a 0AP Action (3.4) , any Fresh
or Spent Unit may play this card and no Spent Check is made.

Blue AP Cost: The full cost of this card must be paid
with CAPs (3.4) to play. Any Spent or Fresh Unit may
play the card and no Spent Check is made.

Green and Blue AP Costs are NEVER affected by ANY
modifiers, including Stress. However, an Action Icon Card will
Stress the Unit(s) that played it.

## 8.7 Mission Icon Cards

Mission Cards determine Mission
Objectives or initiate a Mission 
Scoring, Event, or End at no cost.

When you draw a Mission Card from the
Battle Card Draw Deck, reveal and resolve
it immediately, then discard it and draw 
another Battle Card. 

## 8.8 Artillery Icon Cards

Artillery Icon Weapon Cards that
give a player Off-Board Artillery
(OBA) support at no cost.

You will plan and resolve your Artillery Strikes
during the Pre-Round Sequence (9.4) .

The Artillery’s Firepower and Area of Effect
is listed on each Artillery Card.

## 8.9 Battle Icons

Battle Icons printed on the bottom of a card indicate
special ways a card may be used:

Hidden Unit may take this card's Action and remain
hidden, except if revealed through movement (11.5) .

Groups may play a card as part of a Group Action
(10.1) .

High Explosive (HE): Resolve the Attack against
the Target's Flank Defense. Air Burst (13.9) .

## Key mechanics (vision pass)

- **8.0/8.1/8.2:** two card categories — **Battle Cards** and **Weapon Cards** — with an ID number bottom-left. The Mission specifies which cards are in the Battle Card Draw Deck and how many each player draws per Round. **No hand limit**; unplayed cards carry into future Rounds; **Battle Cards are discarded when played**. Weapon Cards (special Explosives, Artillery) are governed by the Mission.
- **8.4 Card Type Icons (top-left):** **Action** (played as an Action), **Bonus** (modifies the Action you take), **Mission** (special events/scoring), **Artillery** (resolved in the Pre-Round Sequence).
- **8.5/8.6 Playing cost — Green vs Blue:**
  - **Green AP Cost:** played by a **specific Unit**; the listed cost **can be reduced by CAPs** (3.3) before the Spent Check. A Fresh Unit becomes Spent if it fails the d10 check; at 0AP (3.4) any Fresh **or Spent** Unit may play with **no Spent Check**.
  - **Blue AP Cost:** the **full cost must be paid with CAPs** (3.4); **any** Unit (Fresh or Spent) may play it with **no Spent Check**.
  - Green/Blue costs are **never affected by any modifier (including Stress)** — but playing an **Action Icon card Stresses** the Unit(s) that played it.
- **8.7 Mission Icon Cards:** resolve **immediately** when drawn (objectives/scoring/event/end), at no cost; then discard and draw another Battle Card.
- **8.8 Artillery Icon Cards:** give Off-Board Artillery (OBA) at no cost; planned/resolved in the Pre-Round Sequence (9.4); FP and Area of Effect printed on the card.
- **8.9 Battle Icons (bottom of card):** **Hidden Unit** may use the card's Action and stay hidden (unless revealed by movement, 11.5); **Groups** may play it as part of a Group Action (10.1); **High Explosive (HE)** resolves vs the Target's **Flank Defense** (Air Burst, 13.9). *Individual card text is on rulebook p.37.*



## Example (red box)

**Deck construction:** A Mission calling for Battle Cards **01 through 13** builds a deck containing **all copies** of each — e.g. all four copies of Card 01, eight copies of Card 02, and so on.

## Card Catalog (transcribed from rulebook p.36-37)

Individual card text, previously only referenced as "not transcribed" above, was transcribed directly
from `docs/SoS3 Rulebook v70 single pages.pdf` (pages 36-38) via `pdftotext -layout` — the user
explicitly authorized reading the PDF for this one build (overriding this repo's earlier standing
"never read the PDF" rule; see `CLAUDE.md` §0 for the current instruction). This is a one-time source;
this transcription is now the committed, authoritative reference — do not re-open the PDF for Cards
questions, read this section instead.

**Type icon methodology** (§8.4's Action/Bonus/Mission/Artillery, not always explicit in the extracted
text): a card whose text ends "...then draw a new Battle Card" is **Mission**-type; the two Divisional
Artillery cards are **Artillery**-type (explicit); everything else is **Action**-type if the card's
effect is performed *through a specific Unit's Turn* (the card substitutes for that Unit's Action) and
**Bonus**-type if it's reactive/passive/global and doesn't consume a Turn. Each call below is a
judgment call, not a re-transcription of an icon graphic — flagged inline wherever genuinely unclear.

**Cost color methodology**: §8.6's Green (AP cost, CAP-reducible, Spent-Check-gated, Fresh-unit-only
unless reduced to 0AP) vs Blue (fully CAP-paid, any Fresh-or-Spent Unit, never a Spent Check) is
conveyed by ink color on the physical card, which text extraction cannot recover, and no page-image
tool was available in this environment to inspect it visually (checked `pdftoppm`/`pdftocairo`/
ImageMagick/ghostscript/`mutool` — none installed). **For every card with a nonzero cost, the color
below is a best-guess default, not a confirmed fact — correct by glancing at the physical card or PDF.**
Zero-cost cards are unaffected (0AP behaves identically under either color: any Unit, no Spent Check),
so no color guess is load-bearing for them.

A card ID's trailing "(x N)" is the number of physical copies included in the deck whenever that ID is
selected for a Mission (§8.1) — this is the `count` field consumed by deck construction, not a
per-Mission draw count (that's the Mission's own `drawPerRound`, §9.8).

### Battle Icons (§8.9) applied to the catalog above

- **Hidden** (may take this card's Action and remain Hidden, except if revealed through movement,
  §11.5): none of the extracted card texts explicitly print this icon in a way distinguishable from
  ordinary effect text — no card above is marked `battleIcons.hidden` by default. (Card V07 Concealed
  Fire is a *related but distinct* always-on Hidden-Units modifier, not this per-play icon — see its
  row above.) Flag and correct per-card once the physical icon can be visually confirmed.
- **Group** (may be played as part of a Group Action, §10.1): Card 02 Command Action and Card 04 Rally
  Up! explicitly say "...or Group" in their own effect text — marked `battleIcons.group: true`. No
  other card's text mentions Groups explicitly.
- **High Explosive (HE)** (resolves vs the Target's Flank Defense, Air Burst §13.9): both Divisional
  Artillery cards (W06/W07) are HE by rule (§13.9 applies to all Artillery), marked
  `battleIcons.he: true`. No Battle/Veteran card's text claims HE.

### Weapon-card restrictions (`restrictedTo`)

- W01 Grenades, W03 Demolitions: German Foot Units only.
- W02 Molotov Cocktail: Soviet Foot Units only.
- W04/W05 Airplane cards, W06/W07 Divisional Artillery: nation-specific by card ID (German vs Soviet
  variant), not a Unit-kind restriction.
