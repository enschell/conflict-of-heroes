## 13.1 Direct Attacks

A Direct Attack follows the rules for a normal Fire Zone
31 attack, except that a Mortar Unit may only attack a Target
Hex that is not closer its Minimum Range.
Mortars MAY conduct Close Combat with a −2AR CC Penalty
(6.9) .

## 13.2 Indirect Attacks

A valid Target Hex for an Indirect Attack must be:
• Within the Mortar Unit’s Arc of Fire
• Within LOS of a Mortar Spotter Hex (13.3)
• Not closer than the Mortar Unit’s Minimum Range

## 13.3 Spotter Hexes for Indirect LOS

Select any Hex that is within 2 Hexes and clear LOS of the
Mortar Unit to be its Spotter Hex.
For an Indirect Attack, a Mortar Unit establishes LOS and
any Elevation Combat Bonus (12.3) from its Spotter Hex,
rather than from its own Hex.
A Mortar Unit always uses its own Hex to determine whether
a Target Hex is within its Min and Max Range.
Hidden Mortars making Indirect Attacks remain hidden if they
are not in LOS of any enemy Units.
Mortar Units sent out spotter teams to direct their attacks.
Spotters had to be relatively nearby to signal or call
instructions to the mortar team, since very few teams had
radios at that time.

## 13.5 Plan an OBA Strike

Activate an OBA Card during the Pre-Round Sequence to
plan an OBA Strike. Secretly note the Target Hex by writing
it down or by marking the Hex on a Planning Map.
The planned OBA Strike will be resolved in the next Pre-

If the Mission gives no restrictions about where OBA may be
targeted, you may select any Target Hex on the Map.

## 13.6 Resolve an OBA Strike

After planning new OBA Strikes, resolve any Strikes that were
planned on the previous Round.
To resolve an OBA Strike:
1. Place the Artillery Marker on the Target
Hex, with the arrow pointing any direction.
2. Resolve a Drift Check.
3. Resolve all OBA Attacks.

Artillery was usually fired from kilometers away at pre-
marked targets and was either called in by radio, field

## Key mechanics (vision pass)

- **13.0 Mortar Units** (teams of 2–6) fire **High Explosive**. The counter shows a **Direct Attack Cost** and an **Indirect Attack Cost in parentheses**, plus a **Minimum Range** and max Range (e.g. "3–16").
- **13.1 Direct Attack:** normal Fire-Zone attack, except it may **only** target a hex **not closer than its Minimum Range**. Mortars **may** do Close Combat at **−2AR**.
- **13.2 Indirect Attack** — valid target must be: within the Mortar's **Arc of Fire**, within **LOS of a Mortar Spotter Hex** (13.3), and **not closer than Minimum Range**.
- **13.3 Spotter Hex:** any hex **within 2 hexes and in clear LOS** of the Mortar. For an Indirect Attack, LOS and any **Elevation Combat Bonus (12.3)** are taken **from the Spotter Hex**, but **Min/Max Range is always measured from the Mortar's own hex**. A Hidden Mortar firing indirectly **stays hidden** if it isn't in any enemy's LOS.

## Example (red box) — Indirect Mortar Attack

A Soviet Mortar on L0 behind an L2 Hill fires indirectly at a German HMG. **Spotter Hex (A)** is on the L2 hilltop (within 2 hexes, clear LOS); being on L2 it sees **over** the intervening L1 Woods, and an Open hex past the Woods keeps the target **out of a Blind Spot**. Target is **7 hexes** away — past the 3-hex Minimum, within Range. Mortar rounds are **HE, so the HMG's Flank Defense applies (13.9)** → `10 Flank = 10DR`. AR = `3 FP + 1AR (Spotter on high ground, 13.3) = 4AR`. `10 − 4 = 6`; roll 8 → Hit, HMG draws a Hit Marker. Then a **4AP Indirect** Spent Check; roll 2 → Spent, Stressed.

### Off-Board Artillery (OBA)
- **13.4:** OBA = **Artillery Weapon Cards** available in specific Missions; planned in the **Pre-Round Sequence (9.4)** and resolved a Round later.
- **13.5 Plan an OBA Strike:** activate an OBA card in the Pre-Round Sequence, **secretly note the Target Hex**; it resolves in the **next** Pre-Round Sequence. Absent Mission limits, any hex may be targeted.
- **13.6 Resolve:** (1) place the **Artillery Marker** on the target (arrow any direction); (2) **Drift Check**; (3) resolve all OBA Attacks.
- **13.7 Drift Check:** roll **1d6 ≥ Drift Check Number** (German **3**, Soviet **4**), modifiable by up to 2 CAPs. **Success → lands on target.** **Failure → the marker drifts by the failed roll's value in hexes**, with a second **1d6 for direction** (per the marker's numbered arrows; also CAP-modifiable).
- **13.8 OBA Attacks:** using the card's Firepower, roll a **separate Attack for every Unit in the marker's hex and all surrounding hexes — including friendly Units.**
- **13.9 Mortar/Artillery combat modifiers:**
  - Indirect mortar fire takes **Elevation Modifiers from the Spotter Hex** (not its own).
  - **High Explosive (HE):** mortar/artillery attacks **always resolve vs the Target's Flank Defense** (soft and armored).
  - **Air Burst:** Units with **red Flank Defense lose the Heavy Woods +2DR** bonus when hit by HE mortar/artillery.

## Example (red box) — Drift Check

The Soviets need **1d6 ≥ 4** but roll a **3** → miss; the OBA **drifts 3 hexes** (equal to the rolled value). They roll a second 1d6 for the drift **direction**.

## Example (red box) — full OBA Strike (planning → drift → friendly fire)

Germans plan a Strike on hex **4-J08** (written secretly) predicting where the Soviet convoy will be. Two Rounds later they place the Artillery Marker on J08 and roll the **Drift Check**: need **3+**, roll **2** → drifts **2 hexes** (could have spent 2 CAPs to drop the number to 1 for a guaranteed hit). Direction roll = **2** → the marker's "2" arrow sends it **NE two hexes onto their own HMG**. Because artillery is **HE**, the HMG uses **Flank 10 Defense**; the card's red **5 FP** → `10 − 5 = 5` Hit Number; Germans spend 2 CAPs to **raise** it to 7 (to *avoid* hitting their own Unit); roll 3 → miss.
