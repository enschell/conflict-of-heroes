## 6.0 Resolve an Attack

The Unit selected to execute an Attack Action is called the
Attacker. Any Unit or Units occupying the Target Hex are
called the Targets.
After choosing a Target Hex in the Attacker’s Fire Zone,
resolve a separate Attack against each Target in the Hex (6.9) .
To execute an Attack, follow these steps in order:
1. Determine the Target’s Defense Rating (DR)
2. Determine the Attacker’s Attack Rating (AR)
3. Calculate the Hit Number (DR − AR)
4. Resolve a 2d6 Attack Roll against the Hit Number

## 6.7 Range AR Modifiers

The Attacker’s AR may be affected by a Range Modifier
depending on its distance from the Target.
• Long Range Penalty: −2AR if the distance between the
Attacker and its Target Hex exceeds its Range (5.3) .
• Short Range Bonus: +3AR if the Attacker is adjacent to
its Target Hex.
• Close Combat: +4AR if the Attacker is in its Target Hex (6.10) .
Crewed Units have a −2AR penalty in CC (6.11) .

## 6.8 Hit Number & Attack Roll

The Attack’s Hit Number is determined by subtracting the
Attacker’s Attack Rating from the Target’s Defense Rating.
Hit Number = DR − AR
A 2d6 Attack Roll equal to or higher than
the Hit Number results in a Hit (7.0) .
Remember that you may spend up to 2 CAPs to lower the
Hit Number, but you must do so before rolling! (3.2)

## 6.10 Close Combat (CC)

A Unit occupying the same Hex as an enemy Unit is
considered being in Close Combat.
• It may only attack another Unit in that Close Combat Hex.
It may not fire at Targets outside of its CC Hex.
• If there are stacked enemy Units in the Close Combat Hex,
it does not Attack each of them but instead must choose a
single Target to attack.

## Key mechanics (vision pass) — attack resolution

**6.0 Resolve an Attack.** The Unit taking the Attack Action is the **Attacker**; Units in the Target Hex are **Targets**. Resolve a **separate Attack against each Target** in the hex (6.9). Steps:
1. Determine the Target's **Defense Rating (DR)**
2. Determine the Attacker's **Attack Rating (AR)**
3. **Hit Number = DR − AR**
4. Resolve a **2d6 Attack Roll** vs. the Hit Number

**6.1 Defense Rating.** `DR = Target's Defense + DR Modifiers`. Defense numbers are in the **bottom-right** corner. Use the Target's higher **Front Defense** unless attacked from the **Flank** (6.3).

**6.2 Soft vs Armored (by Defense color).** **Red Defense = Soft Target**; **Blue Defense = Armored Target**.

**6.3 Flank Attack.** An Attack **initiated from outside the Target's Arc of Fire** is a Flank Attack → use the Target's lower **Flank Defense** instead of Front Defense.

**6.4 Defensive & Exposed Terrain DR modifiers:**
| Terrain | DR modifier |
|---|---|
| Light Woods | +1 DR |
| Heavy Woods | +2 DR |
| Wood Buildings | +1 DR |
| Stone Buildings | +2 DR |
| Walls | +1 DR |
| Water (Foot Units, **Exposed**) | −1 DR |

**6.5 Walls.** **+1 DR** Wall Cover if the Attack **crosses a Wall** bordering the Target's hex. Walls **do not block LOS**, and Wall Cover **does not apply vs Mortar/Artillery fire**.

**6.6 Attack Rating.** `AR = Attacker's Firepower (FP) + AR Modifiers`. FP numbers are in the **bottom-left** corner. **Red FP attacks Soft Targets; Blue FP attacks Armored Targets** — i.e. **Attacker's Firepower color must match the Target's Defense color**.

**6.7 Range AR modifiers:**
- **Long Range Penalty −2AR** if distance **exceeds** Range (5.3).
- **Short Range Bonus +3AR** if Attacker is **adjacent** to the Target Hex.
- **Close Combat +4AR** if Attacker is **in** the Target Hex (6.10); **Crewed Units take −2AR in CC** (6.11).

**6.8 Hit Number & Attack Roll.** `Hit Number = DR − AR`. A **2d6 roll ≥ Hit Number = a Hit** (7.0). You may spend **up to 2 CAPs to lower the Hit Number, before rolling** (3.2).

**6.9 Attacking Stacked Units.** If multiple Units share the Target Hex (4.3), make a **separate Attack Roll against each** (friendly and enemy). It is **one Attack Action → only one d10 Spent Check**. With mixed Soft+Armored Targets, use the **matching FP color** against each. Each Target's own facing sets Front vs Flank Defense. CAPs spent to reduce a Hit Number affect **only one 2d6 roll** (up to 2 CAPs each).

## Examples (red boxes)

**Attack Action (German MG34 → Soviet Rifles):**
- DR: attacking within the Rifles' Arc of Fire → use red **12 Front Defense**; +1DR Light Woods (6.4) → **13DR**.
- AR: Rifles are a Soft Target (red Defense) → MG34 uses red **4 Firepower**; normal range, no modifier → **4AR**.
- **Hit Number = 13 − 4 = 9.** Spend 1 CAP → Hit Number 8. Roll 2d6 = 7 → **miss**.
- MG34's Attack cost 2AP; passes its Spent Check, stays Fresh, Stressed.

**Flank Attack (Soviet Rifles → German PaK 38, who is Spent in a Wood Building):**
- DR: attack from **outside the PaK's Arc of Fire** → use **10 Flank Defense** (6.3); +1DR Wood Building (6.4) → **11DR**. (Being Spent doesn't change Defense.)
- AR: PaK's Flank Defense is red (Soft) → Rifles use red **3 Firepower**; **adjacent → +3AR Short Range** (6.7) → **6AR**.
- **Hit Number = 11 − 6 = 5.** Spend the max **2 CAPs** → Hit Number 3. Roll 2d6 = 6 → **Hit!**
- Rifles then spend 1 CAP to lower their 4AP Attack Cost to 3AP; roll a 3 on the Spent Check → **fail → Spent**, and Stressed.

**6.10 Close Combat (CC).** A Unit in the **same hex** as an enemy is in Close Combat. It may **only** attack a Unit **in that CC hex** (cannot fire outside it); if enemies are stacked there, it must choose a **single** Target.
- CC modifiers: **+4AR** to the Attacker; Targets defend with **Flank Defense**; Foot Units and Crewed Units still gain Terrain DR Modifiers (6.4).

**6.11 Crewed Units.** Marked with a **white field** behind one or both Firepower values (MGs, Mortars (13.0), Field Guns (16.7)). FP on a white field **does not get the +4AR CC bonus — instead suffers −2AR** in CC.

**6.12 Withdraw from Close Combat.** Initiating CC needs a **Move Action**, so the attacker must wait a Turn — the opponent gets the first chance to attack or withdraw. If you withdraw rather than attack: on the Turn after an enemy moved into your hex, you **may not** advance your Units into the hex the enemy previously occupied, nor either of the **two hexes adjacent** to that previously enemy-occupied hex.

**Stacked Targets (red box).** Soviet Rifles fire into a hex with German Rifles + an MG34 — a separate roll vs each:
- (A) vs Rifles, from their flank → red **11 Flank Defense**, Open Terrain; Soft → red **3FP**. `11 − 3 = 8`; roll 6 → miss.
- (B) vs MG34, facing the Soviets → red **12 Front Defense**; Soft → red **3FP**. `12 − 3 = 9`; roll 10 → Hit.
- 4AP Attack, **one** d10 Spent Check for both rolls; roll 5 → Fresh, Stressed.

**Close Combat (red box).** (A) Soviet Rifles move into Light Woods 1-J06 (+1DR) to initiate CC with a Spent German HMG. (B) The HMG is Spent and can't afford 0AP to fire, so Germans move a Rifles '41 in to join. The Soviet Rifles may attack only **one** of the two Germans (6.10) or withdraw (not into the red-highlighted hexes, 6.12). (C) They attack the HMG, which defends with its **10 Flank Defense regardless of facing** (CC). HMG: `10 + 1DR Light Woods = 11DR`. Rifles: `3 FP + 4AR CC = 7AR`. `11 − 7 = 4`; roll 7 → Hit.
