## 12.4 LOS between different level Hexes

1. Determine the Elevation Levels of the Attack and Target
Hexes. Whichever is highest is the High Ground Hex.
2. LOS is established, if no intervening Hex is of equal or
higher Level than the High Ground Hex.
LOS rules apply in both directions. If Hex A has LOS to Hex
B, then Hex B always has LOS to Hex A.
The Plateau Effect
LOS between Hexes ‘A’ and ‘C’:
L1 Hex C is determined to be the High Ground Hex.

## Key mechanics (vision pass)

- **12.0 Elevation:** every hex has an Elevation from **Level 0 Ground** up to **Level 2 Hill (▲▲)**; no symbol = Level 0. The Elevation printed by the hex coordinate applies to the **whole hex**.
- **12.1 Hills:** **▲ = Level 1 (L1)**, **▲▲ = Level 2 (L2)**. A Hill hex can have any Terrain Type.
- **12.2 Elevation Move Cost Penalties** (Foot Units & Field Guns moving **into** a higher hex):
  - **Sloping (1 level):** Ascending **+1AP**; Descending **no penalty**.
  - **Steep (2 levels):** Ascending **or** Descending **+2AP**; **Impassable to all Wheeled and Tracked vehicles**.
  - **Roads do NOT negate Elevation penalties.**
- **12.3 Elevation Combat Modifiers:** **+1AR** if the **Attacker** is higher than the Target; **+1DR** if the **Target** is higher than the Attacker.

## Examples (red boxes)

**Changing Elevation (penalties only):** L0→L1 ascending **+1AP** (A); L1→L2 ascending **+1AP** (B); L2→L1 descending **+0AP** (C); L1→L0 descending **+0AP** (D).

**Steep Terrain:** German Rifles L0→L2 Hill: **+2AP** Steep penalty → total **3AP**. Later, L2→L0 back down: also **+2AP**.

**Roads and Hills:** Rifles L0→L1 Hill **following a Road**: the Road **does not** remove the **+1AP** Sloping penalty → **2AP**. Next Turn, continuing along the Road into Light Woods while **staying on L1**: no Elevation penalty, but **+1AP Stress** → **2AP**.

### LOS and elevation
- **12.4 LOS between different levels:** the higher of the two hexes is the **High Ground Hex**. LOS exists **only if no intervening hex is at an equal or higher Level** than the High Ground Hex (reversible).
  - **Plateau Effect:** an intervening hex at the **same level** as the High Ground Hex **blocks** LOS (e.g. L1→L1 across an L1 hex = blocked). An L2 High Ground Hex **can** see past intervening L1 hexes, but **not** to a second L2 hex beyond it.
- **12.5 LOS-Blocking Terrain adds +1 Level for LOS purposes only:** Buildings on L0 → an **L1** LOS-blocker; Woods on an L1 Hill → an **L2** LOS-blocker.
- **12.6 Blind Spots:** LOS-Blocking Terrain creates a **Blind Spot Hex directly behind it** from the High Ground Hex's view. A High Ground Hex **never** has LOS to a Blind Spot Hex (and vice versa), regardless of its elevation.

## Examples (red boxes)

**LOS from an L1 Hill:** a German MG34 on an L1 Hill **cannot** see Soviet Rifles on L0 when an **L1 Hill hex sits between** them (plateau), but **can** see an ATG on L0 with nothing of equal/higher level in between.

**Elevation + Blocking Terrain:** the same L1 MG34 **cannot** see Rifles through intervening Woods (L0 Woods counts as an **L1** blocker); from an **L2** Hill it **would** see them.

**Blind Spots:** an L2 Hill (Hex 1) sees the "green" hexes but **not** the "red" Blind-Spot hexes behind L1 blocking terrain (Woods hexes 10 & 4, Building hexes 6 & 8). Side view: Hex 1 sees 2, 3, 4, 6; L1 Hex 3 sees Hex 4 but nothing beyond, since the Woods act as an L1 blocker at the same level (plateau).
