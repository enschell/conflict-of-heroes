# 1.0 Game Components

> Source: *Awakening the Bear* (Conflict of Heroes, 3rd ed.) rulebook, printed p.5. Transcribed from the page image (component photos + setup).

**Apply when:** identifying the physical components by name, the Unit counter layout, hex notation, or the General Setup sequence.

**Subsections:** 1.0, 1.1

---

## Game components (names)

- **Maps 1–5** (geomorphic map boards). *Map 6 is an expansion Marsh Map; Maps 7–10 ship with* Storms of Steel.
- **Two d6 Battle Dice** (standard six-sided, used for Attack Rolls and number checks).
- **Two custom d10 Spent Dice** (used for the Spent Check).
- **Four Stress Markers.**
- **Counter Sheets**, containing:
  - **Hit Markers** (Soft Target and Armored Target types)
  - **Soviet Units**
  - **German Units**
- **1× Round and Victory Track** sheet — the **Mission Round Track** (spaces 1–6) and the **Victory Points (VPs) Track**.
- **4× CAPs Tracks** — each a **Command Action Points (CAPs)** track sheet, including the **Spent Check** reference (Action Cost vs. Spent Chance 20%–100%).
- **Cards:**
  - **50 Battle Cards** (Nos. 01–24)
  - **5 Weapon Cards** (Nos. W01–W05)
  - *Example card faces shown: Command Action / "One Free Action", "Seek Cover!" / Hasty Defense, "Careful Aiming" / +2FP Bonus, Divisional Artillery.*

### Unit counter layout
Each Unit has a **symbol** and a **unique Unit number** in the top center. The counter shows an **Attack Cost** (top-left corner) and a **Move Cost** (top-right corner) — see 2.4 — plus its combat ratings (Firepower / Range / Defense). Example counters pictured: **Rifles '41** (Unit 01) and **Pz III J** (Unit 52).

## 1.0 Setup  *(General Setup sequence)*

1. **Choose Sides:** Choose which Mission to play and which side each player will command.
2. **Place Maps:** Place Maps as described in the Mission.
3. **Set Up Units:** Place Units and Control Markers on the setup Hexes listed in the Mission Brief. These Units must face the direction shown on the Mission Map.
   - Hexes are identified by **(Map #)–(Column Letter & Row #)**. Example: **1-E05** designates Map 1, Hex E05.
4. **Place CAPs Markers:** Distribute CAPs Track Sheets to all players. Each player places a CAPs Marker on their starting CAP value as specified in the Mission.
5. **Prepare Battle Draw Deck:** Create the Battle Deck by separating out and shuffling the Battle Cards specified by the Mission (8.1). Each player draws their specified number of Battle Cards.
6. **Place Round and Victory Markers:** Place the Round and Victory Track Sheet near the Map.
   - Place the **Victory Point (VP) Marker** on the VP Track space specified in the Mission.
   - Place the **Mission Round Marker** on the '1' space of the Mission Round Track.
   - Place any **Reinforcement Units** on the Mission Round Track in the space indicating the Round they become available.
7. **Prepare Hit Markers:** Separate the **Soft Target** and **Armored Target** Hit Markers into two Draw Piles. Make sure the yellow effect side of each Marker is face-down.

> **Example (red box):** *Armored Target Hit Markers are not used in early Missions.*

> **Designer note (blue box):** *Awakening the Bear* includes Maps 1–5. Map 6 is an expansion Marsh Map. Maps 7–10 are included in *Storms of Steel*. Missions combining Maps from various releases are at www.AcademyGames.com/AtB.

## 1.1 Units

Conflict of Heroes includes Units that represent infantry squads of 6–12 men, crewed units such as mortars, individual vehicles such as tanks, and many others. Each Unit has a symbol and a unique Unit number in the top center (e.g., **01** above the **Rifles '41** name).

> **Designer note (blue box):** Unit symbols are used in Solo expansions. Unit numbers are for sorting convenience. Unit Summaries are listed on pg. 38.
